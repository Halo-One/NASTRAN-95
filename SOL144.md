# SOL 144, static aeroelastic trim, in nastran95ase

COSMIC NASTRAN never had a static aeroelastic solution: its aeroelastic rigid
formats are AERO 9 (blade cyclic modal flutter), AERO 10 (modal flutter, MSC's
SOL 145) and AERO 11 (modal aeroelastic response, SOL 146). MSC added static
aeroelasticity (SOL 21, then SOL 144) in the 1980s. This note is the theory
the fork's SOL 144 is built on and how it is built. The sources are the MSC
Nastran 2025.1 Aeroelastic Analysis User's Guide (Rodden & Johnson; chapter 2
"Static Aeroelasticity", chapter 5 "Output Features", chapter 7 examples), the
2025.1 Quick Reference Guide (AEROS, AESTAT, AESURF, AELIST, AELINK, TRIM,
SUPORT1) and the 2025.1 DMAP Programmer's Guide (ADG, ASG, SDP); all three are
public PDFs on Hexagon's documentation site (`https://documentation-be.hexagon.com/bundle/<B>/raw/resource/enus/<B>.pdf`,
`<B>` = `MSC_Nastran_2025.1_Aeroelastic_Analysis_User_Guide` and so on).

## The equations (MSC's, in MSC's notation)

Aerodynamics, quasi-steady (reduced frequency zero):

    w_j = D1_jk u_k + D_jx u_x                downwash at the j points
    f_j = q A_jj^-1 w_j                        pressures (A: the AIC, w = A f/q)
    P_k = S_kj f_j                             forces at the k points
    Q_aa = G_ka^T S_kj A_jj^-1 D1_jk G_ka      (2-107)
    Q_ax = G_ka^T S_kj A_jj^-1 D_jx            (2-108)

`u_x` are the "aerodynamic extra points", the trim variables: the AESTAT rigid
body motions in the reference frame of AEROS RCSID (ANGLEA = R2 rotation,
SIDES = R3 rotation, ROLL/PITCH/YAW = R1/R2/R3 rates as pb/2V, qc/2V, rb/2V,
URDD1..6 = accelerations of the reference point, divided by PARAM AUNITS) and
the AESURF control rotations (radians, right-handed about the y axis of the
surface's CID). Accelerations make no downwash. D_jx is the downwash of a unit
value of each: a rigid rotation phi of the vehicle (or of the surface's boxes
about the hinge) gives `w_j = -n_j . (phi x V_hat)`, a rotation rate Omega
gives `w_j = -n_j . (Omega x (r_j - r_ref)) / V`, with n_j the box normal and
r_j the box's 3/4-chord collocation point. (NASTRAN-95's D1JK and D2JK give
exactly this when fed the k-set motion of the rigid body: D1JK picks the box
pitch, D2JK the plunge velocity at the box centre plus pitch rate x chord/4.)

Structure, a-set, partitioned into the SUPORT set r and the rest l:

    [K_aa - q Q_aa] u_a + M_aa u''_a = q Q_ax u_x + P_a        (2-109)
    D = -K_ll^-1 K_lr        (rigid body modes, DM)
    m_r = M_rr + M_rl D + D^T M_lr + D^T M_ll D                (MR)
    u''_l = D u''_r   (quasi-steady),  u''_r = TR^T TRX u_x     (2-113, 2-115)

TR transforms reference-point accelerations to the r dofs (and r-dof forces to
the reference point); TRX picks the URDD entries of u_x.

**Restrained** (u_r = 0, the displacements relative to the SUPORT point, which
is what SOL 144 prints): with K^a_ll = K_ll - q Q_ll,

    u_l = (K^a_ll)^-1 [ q Q_lx u_x - (M_ll D + M_lr) u''_r + P_l ]     (2-116)

and the r rows premultiplied by D^T give six equilibrium equations

    ZZX u_x = PZ                                                       (2-117)

augmented with the TRIM card's fixed values (IP u_x = Y) and the AELINK rows
(AEL u_x = 0), square when nr + nfixed + nlinks = nx (2-120). In words: the
aerodynamic force on the restrained elastic vehicle, summed through the rigid
body modes to the SUPORT point, equals m_r u''_r.

**Stability derivatives**, per unit u_x, made non-dimensional by 1/(qS) and
1/(qSb), 1/(qSc), 1/(qSb) for CMX, CMY, CMZ (NDIM, 2-122/2-123), at the
reference point (TR):

| column | what |
|---|---|
| RIGID UNSPLINED | `q SRKT^T S_kj A^-1 D_jx`: the k-set box forces summed straight to the reference point |
| RIGID SPLINED | `q D_a^T Q_ax`, D_a = [D; I]: the same forces after the spline, summed through the rigid body modes (KSAZX, 2-136); the two agree when the spline transfers every box |
| ELASTIC RESTRAINED | `KRZX = ZZX - m_r TR^T TRX` (2-121): the restrained elastic aero force; for URDD columns the aero force of the deformation the inertia loads make |
| ELASTIC UNRESTRAINED | the mean-axis derivatives (2-125..2-134): deformation orthogonal to the rigid body modes in the mass metric, `D^T M (u) = 0`, so the result does not depend on where the SUPORT is; zero for the URDD columns |
| INERTIAL RESTRAINED | `m_r TR^T TRX`: the rigid body inertia of the URDD columns (CZ of URDD3 is weight/(qS) when AUNITS = 1/g) |
| INERTIAL UNRESTRAINED | the inertial load that balances the free vehicle, m_r u''_r per unit u_x: equal to the elastic unrestrained column (Simcenter prints the two to 6 digits of each other) |

The REF. COEFF. row (the intercept) holds the derivatives of the user
downwash/pressure inputs (W2GJ, FA2J, AEDW/AEPRESS); zero here, since the
front end supports none of those.

**Unrestrained, how it is solved here.** With u_l = D u_r + v_l the mean axis
condition is `u_r = -m_r^-1 (M_ll D + M_lr)^T v_l`, the l rows give
`K^a_ll v_l = q Q_lx u_x + q Q_la D_a u_r - (M_ll D + M_lr) u''_r`, and the r
rows `m_r u''_r = q D_a^T Q_ax u_x + q D_a^T Q_aa D_a u_r + q D_a^T Q_al v_l`.
Everything is linear in (u_r, u''_r) once the restrained solves for the three
right hand sides are known, so it is a 12 x 12 system per trim variable.

## How it is computed (the s-set)

The aerodynamic matrices touch only the a-set dofs the splines reach (the
"s-set": the non-zero rows of G_ka). With C_ls = K_ll^-1 E_s (E_s injects the
s dofs into l; one FBS of the structural factor with ns right hand sides) and
C_ss its s rows, every restrained solve is

    (K_ll - q E Q_ss E^T) u = f
    u_s = (I - q C_ss Q_ss)^-1 (K_ll^-1 f)_s,   u = K_ll^-1 (f + q E Q_ss u_s)

so per dynamic pressure there is one dense ns x ns LU (LAPACK, double
precision), and K_ll^-1 is applied only through FBS of NASTRAN's own factor.

## What is built (the DMAP and the module)

SOL 144 has no rigid format: the front end writes the solution as an
`APP DMAP` program (msc/msctrim.c, `msc_trim_dmap`), the DISP 2 (inertia
relief) structure sequence with AERO 10's aero modules:

    GP1..GP4, EMG/EMA, MCE1/MCE2, SCE1, SMP1    the a-set and its SUPORT r-set
    RBMG1-4                                       KLL, LLL (factor), DM, MR
    DPD, APD                                      aero boxes (ACPT, BGPA, ...)
    GI (USET purged) -> GTKG, GI -> GTKA         the spline, g-set and a-set
    AMG                                           AJJL, SKJ, D1JK at k ~ 0
    AETRIM mode 1                                 ES (s-set injection), DG (g-set rigid body modes)
    FBS LLL,,ES/CLS ; FBS LLL,,MDL/XM ...        C_ls, K^-1 (M D + M_lr), MGG DG
    AETRIM mode 2                                 the trim: PLA, UDDT, PGT
    MPYAD, FBS LLL                                u_l = K_ll^-1 (PLA - MDL u''_r)
    SDR1, SDR2, OFP                               DISPLACEMENT and OLOAD per subcase

`AETRIM` is a new DMAP module (mis/aetrim.f: the GINO reading and the print;
msc/mscaest.c: the algebra, LAPACK through OpenBLAS). The TRIM, AEROS, AESTAT,
AESURF, AELIST, AELINK cards and PARAM AUNITS never reach NASTRAN-95's input
file processor: the front end parses them and hands them to the module in
memory (both run in one process).

The doublet lattice is NASTRAN-95's own (AMG, the same kernel as SOL 145/146).
COSMIC's IFP rejects k = 0 on MKAERO1, so the front end writes MKAERO1 with the
TRIM Machs and a reduced frequency small enough that the oscillatory part of
the kernel is below single precision (the real part of AJJ is used).

## Output

Per subcase, in MSC's print layout:

* `N O N - D I M E N S I O N A L   S T A B I L I T Y   A N D   C O N T R O L
  D E R I V A T I V E   C O E F F I C I E N T S`, the six columns above
  (read by `read_nastran_trim`);
* `A E R O S T A T I C   D A T A   R E C O V E R Y   O U T P U T   T A B L E S`
  with `AEROELASTIC TRIM VARIABLES` (ID, label, type, status, value);
* `D I S P L A C E M E N T   V E C T O R` (relative to the SUPORT point,
  u_r = 0, as MSC and Simcenter print it) and `L O A D   V E C T O R` (OLOAD:
  aerodynamic minus inertial loads at the g-set grids, which sum to zero for a
  trimmed free vehicle).
* `N O N - D I M E N S I O N A L   H I N G E   M O M E N T ...` per AESURF: the
  moment of its boxes' forces about the hinge line (the y axis of its CID, through
  the origin), rigid, elastic restrained and unrestrained, over q CREFC CREFS.
* `APRES` / `AEROF`: the boxes' pressure coefficients and pressures, and their k-set
  forces (T3, R2 in the box frame), at trim.

## Conventions settled from the documents

* AELINK: `u_D + sum C_i u_i = 0` (QRG 2025.1, and DMAP Guide ASG: the AEL rows
  have zero right hand side). An integer ID is the TRIM set it applies to;
  0 or ALWAYS applies to every subcase. Simcenter accepts only the integer form.
* TRIM AEQR multiplies the dynamic pressure of the aeroelastic feedback
  (Q_aa); 0 is a rigid trim.
* AUNITS divides every URDD on the TRIM card (accelerations in g for 1/g).
* SUPORT must give six independent rigid body dofs (a free-flying full model);
  half models (SYMXZ != 0) and ACSID != 0 are not supported yet.
